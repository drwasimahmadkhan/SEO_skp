Website readiness audit platform

SEO_skp crawls a website and reports separate SEO, AEO, and GEO readiness scores with explainable issues and practical recommendations.

Doctor metaphor: the system performs the check-up and writes the prescription. Applying fixes remains the website owner’s responsibility.

1. Problem

Small business owners often cannot tell what is wrong with their site. Tools such as Screaming Frog or Ahrefs can be expensive or technical, and many focus only on traditional SEO. In our testing, three apps gave scores of 97, 92, and 48 for the same site — with little explanation of how each score was built.

3. The three pillars

SEO — can search engines find and rank the site?

Titles, meta descriptions (missing vs empty), headings, images/ALT, links, HTTPS, robots, sitemap, schema, load time.

AEO — does the site answer questions clearly?

Beyond “has an FAQ?”, the AEO Answerability Analyzer generates visitor questions from page text only, then marks each answer as Clear, Partial, or No answer. Missing answers get rewrite suggestions with placeholders such as [your confirmed lead time] — never invented facts.

GEO — can AI tools understand and cite the site?

Checks AI bot access, Organization/LocalBusiness schema, trust signals, specificity, brand consistency, and builds an Entity Graph: what an AI would learn from the site alone — and what it would miss.

AEO, GEO, and LLMO are industry labels, not official Google ranking systems. We call our checks best-practice signals, not ranking factors.

6. Scores & report
  1. Validate URL format, reachability, and HTTPS preference.
  2. Crawl from the homepage (robots.txt, sitemap.xml, internal links, page cap ~25).
  3. Extract title, meta, headings, images, links, canonical, viewport, robots meta, JSON-LD, body text.
  4. Run SEO / AEO / GEO rule checks; failed checks become issues with severity.
  5. NLP: readability, keywords, duplicates, title–content match, intent signals.
  6. Optional local LLM: questions, recommendations, FAQ/schema drafts (JSON, grounded in page text).
  7. Store scores; show progress, issues, PDF, history, before/after.

9. Evaluation

We measure whether reported issues are correct (true/false positives and misses), not whether our overall number matches another vendor’s score. See EVALUATION.md in the repository root.

Fixture site books.toscrape.com should surface empty meta description, missing canonical, no schema, missing robots.txt, and missing sitemap.xml.

11. Disclaimer

These scores are a readiness assessment only. They are not official Google rankings or AI citation scores. The system audits and recommends; it does not modify websites or guarantee outcomes.
